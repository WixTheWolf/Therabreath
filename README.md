# Future of Freshness - TheraBreath Flavor Playbook

**The Flavor Factory x TheraBreath / Church & Dwight**  
**Monday, November 9, 2026 | 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM ET | Darwin room | Church & Dwight, New Jersey**

This repository contains the working creative, presentation, film, pre-brief, and live workshop experience for the **Future of Freshness** session on November 9, 2026.

The July 2026 capabilities review established The Flavor Factory's operational and partnership foundation. The November workshop moves the conversation forward: **where freshness goes next, which flavor territories are worth pursuing, and how those ideas become a prioritized innovation pipeline for TheraBreath.**

## Workshop objective

Build a practical flavor playbook that helps TheraBreath lead the category rather than follow it.

The session is organized around four questions:

1. **Trends** - Which flavor, sensory, and consumer shifts matter most for oral care?
2. **Territories** - Where can TheraBreath expand the definition of freshness?
3. **Concepts** - Which ideas can create new occasions, reach new consumers, and expand the portfolio?
4. **Pipeline** - What should move now, next, and later?

The goal is not to leave the room with a pile of ideas. The goal is to leave with **priorities, guardrails, owners, and next actions**.

These are working innovation directions, not final product names, claims, or commercial formulations. Final sample selection and formula feasibility remain subject to flavorist review and stability work in the actual TheraBreath base.

## Workshop experience

The November experience is designed as one connected story:

- **Opening film** - sets up the idea that the future of flavor belongs to the brand willing to lead.
- **Future of Freshness presentation** - trends, sensory language, global inspiration, white space, and flavor territories.
- **Blind tasting** - coded samples and live reactions.
- **Co-creation** - teams turn territories into occasions, consumers, and product ideas.
- **The Bench** - a live sensory design exercise.
- **Flavor Playbook** - the room prioritizes what moves now, next, and later.
- **Leave-behind** - the decisions and workshop output become the working playbook after the session.

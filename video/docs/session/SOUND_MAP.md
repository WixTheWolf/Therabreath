# THE FLAVOR RACE: Sound Map (locked cut, 2:49.5)

The picture is locked. This map decides, beat by beat, what the audience should FEEL, which element leads (SFX, MUSIC, DIALOGUE or SILENCE), what we deliberately leave out, and what sound arrives before or survives after each cut.

## The four motifs (our leitmotifs)

| Motif | Sonic recipe | Where it lives | How it evolves |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THERABREATH** | Precision servos, clean pressurized air, smooth high turbine, refined clicks, a faint crystalline ring in its ignition | Lights reveal, READY chime, turbopump spin-up, relights, landing, hatch, tray arm | Starts cold and technical, ends warm and playful (the tray arm "performs") |
| **COMPETITOR** | Rough diesel-like idle, unstable vibration, grinding turbine, abrasive midrange, metal rattle | Floodlit close-up, its turbine at the count, its cough in orbit, the pass, the failure | The same rough vibration that is introduced at 0:19 is what shakes the bolt loose (story told by sound) |
| **FLAVOR / FUTURE** | Water drops, glass resonance, airy swirls, delicate sparkle, a sung crystal tone | First teased in the opening droplet; blooms in the lab; returns as the final note | Industrial world turns organic and warm |
| **SPACE** | Near silence, a barely audible low drone, tiny structural ticks heard through the hull | Burner cutoff, the float, the drift after the explosion, the post-credit screw | Lonely, then funny (the screw) |

## Beat by beat

### I. Mystery (0:00 to 0:08.5)
- **0:00 black.** Feel: lean forward. True silence for 0.4s, then one tiny tick of cold plastic contracting, dry and close. A pressure swell begins below hearing.
- **0:01 sheen on plastic.** Microscopic: frost crystallizing, a faint crackle. Not heard: wind, music, anything large.
- **0:02.6 cap ribs.** A droplet rolls and falls: a crisp plink with a very long tail. The reverb is the clue: this small object lives in an enormous space.
- **0:04.6 beaded shoulder.** The unknown: far away, a giant steel structure groans in the cold, and something pressurized vents. "What is that?"
- **0:06.4 cap against the night.** The world opens: wide night wind, a distant cable pinging a tower. The stereo field widens for the first time.
- **0:08 black.** The wind cuts. In the dark, one massive relay clunks somewhere offscreen. Something is waking up.

### II. The reveal (0:08.5 to 0:23.1)
- **0:08.5 silhouettes.** Night pad: vast and quiet. Relays clack in sequence; a transformer hum rises (pre-lap for the lights).
- **0:11.04 bank one (left).** Contactor slam, lamp ignition thump, buzz settling into a floodlight hum. Panned left. Attack, body, tail.
- **0:12.84 bank two (right).** Same family, farther and bigger, panned right, longer tail.
- **0:14.04 all banks.** The biggest: every contactor at once, the light becomes sound (a crystalline shimmer bloom, the first hint of the TheraBreath motif), and the sub arrives a beat late. No trailer BRAAM. The score enters here and takes the baton.
- **0:15 to 0:19.6 hero hold.** Music leads. Under it: floodlight hum, cryo vapor hissing off both rockets.
- **0:19.6 COMPETITOR.** Its motif arrives: a rough, uneven idle with a metallic rattle, felt more than noticed.
- **0:21.6 the loose bolt.** Everything drops away except the competitor's idle, now muffled, and the bolt rattling in time with it. Sound tells the story the picture only hints at. One small tink.
- **0:23.1 title.** Music leads. A restrained low air push and a soft crystalline resolve. No slam.

### III. Flavor Control (0:26.3 to 0:38.6)
- Dialogue leads. A 1960s control room: warm, small, short reflections; console hum, fans, a distant teletype. The roll call rides a relay-click metronome; the stumble on "Finish" keeps its empty beat and the paper shuffle.
- **0:31.97 READY.** A clean, glassy confirmation chime (TheraBreath tech), not a random beep.
- **0:34.2 to 0:38.6 gauges.** Pressure rising: needle creaks, tanks ping as they chill, propellant rushes through pipes. The room tone thins as the machine takes over.

### IV. The machine wakes (0:38.6 to 0:47.6)
- **0:38.6 "Go for flavor."** Exterior pad, open air with a long environmental tail. Vapor venting from both rockets, the tower creaks, valves actuate. The TheraBreath turbopump spins up: clean and rising. The competitor's: grinding and uneven.
- **0:41.5 "3, 2, 1."** Whine rising, rumble deepening, a heartbeat under each number.
- **0:43.77 the umbilical.** Pneumatic snap and cryo blast, right on "one".
- **0:44.7 the button.** A heavy, solid click.
- **0:45.3 igniters.** Rapid electric snapping; pressure at its peak.
- **0:46.6 black.** CUT TO SILENCE. Every bed gone. After a long beat, one small relay click.

### V. Liftoff (0:47.6 to 1:06.7)
- **0:47.6 ignition.** A violent crack, then combustion blooming into a roar; the sub slams a fraction later. The score is carved down.
- **0:49.7 main liftoff.** Built in layers: sub pressure, mid-bass body, combustion crackle, the gantry and debris rattling, the pad reflecting the roar. As the smoke wall hits, the perspective goes inside the cloud: the roar muffles, then a rushing wall of air.
- **0:54.5 from above.** Farther away: more air, less detail. The score rises and takes the baton.
- **0:56.2 ground level.** Distant-perspective roar rolling across the land, with crackle.
- **1:00.7 cloud punch.** The cloud tears; wind shear.
- **1:03.3 climb.** The air thins: highs fall away, the pitch descends, and the roar narrows toward nothing.

### VI. Space (1:06.7 to 1:24.6)
- **1:06.7 edge of space.** The roar CUTS. From here the engines are heard only through the hull: a low structural hum.
- **1:08.6 burners off.** The rumble winds down, a final hiss, then hot metal ticking as it cools. A precise servo adjusts.
- **1:10.7 the float.** Near silence and a barely audible drone, with one small structural tick. The competitor coughs once (rough, muffled): funny, and foreshadowing.
- **1:13.2 relight.** Igniter clicks, a muffled whump through the hull, clean thrust rising. The score returns.
- **1:14.8 the race.** Music leads. The competitor's grinding engine passes left to right as it shoulders past, with its fanfare.

### VII. The lab: future flavors (1:24.6 to 1:35)
- **1:24.6 ingredients.** Four tiny organic hits: a juicy citrus split, petals fluttering, leaves tumbling, a crisp slice. The score steps back.
- **1:26.7 citrus mist.** A delicate sparkling hiss in a small, bright, glass-lined lab acoustic.
- **1:28.2 the drop.** A hyper-real plink whose glass ring blooms into a sustained tone.
- **1:29.6 aroma takes shape.** Soft swirling air and faint sparkles.
- **1:31.2 light through the vial.** The light becomes sound: a sung crystal tone.
- **1:32.4 sealed.** A precise twist-lock click and a frost bloom.
- **1:33.5 launched.** A pneumatic thoomp rising into a whoosh. Music swells.

### VIII. The failure (1:35 to 1:54.6)
- **1:35 the flavor ring.** Music leads; a soft glitter passes.
- **1:40.6 relight.** The new TheraBreath engine: powerful, with a crystalline edge.
- **1:42 the bolt.** Threads squeak and ratchet as it backs out.
- **1:43.9 sputter.** Misfires, unstable combustion, the hull straining, a low alarm.
- **1:46.5 smoke.** Harsh gas leak hiss, crackle.
- **1:48.8 the label.** An unstable whine rises. The music pulls back; for a fraction of a second, almost silence.
- **1:50.2 the explosion.** A heavy body and a delayed sub, then debris pings. The attack is softened (space); the mass is not.
- **1:52.1 adrift.** Silence. One faint creak as the wreck turns. "Not ideal." Hold the beat.

### IX. The Moon (1:54.6 to 2:17.4)
- Music leads (Mr. Blue Sky). SFX turn small and intimate: soft retro-thruster puffs, a hydraulic sigh as the legs compress, a little settling creak (performance, not slapstick), the foot pad pressing into fine dust under "The Flavor has landed."

### X. Home (2:17.4 to 2:46.1)
- **2:17.4 lift off the Moon.** A clean, warmer ignition.
- **2:22 reentry.** A short burst of plasma roar and buffeting under the song.
- **2:24.75 evening.** Soft breeze, distant birds: the world is gentle now.
- **2:27.7 touchdown at home.** The engines throttle down and spin down, then cooling metal ticks.
- **2:30.9 hatch.** A refined pneumatic hiss.
- **2:34.1 the tray.** The robot arm performs: smooth whirs, a tiny hesitation and correction (the humor), glasses clinking softly.
- **2:41.5 end card.** The song fades; one sung crystal tone (the flavor motif) rings out into silence.

### XI. Post-credit (2:46.1 to 2:49.5)
- Space silence. The screw tumbles closer. A tiny glass tink against the lens.

## Acoustic spaces (reverb is storytelling)
- **Night pad, exterior:** few early reflections, a long environmental tail, slap echoes off the towers
- **Control room:** small, warm, short
- **Lab:** small, bright, glassy
- **Space:** no air; engine and mechanics heard only as low, filtered hull vibration
- **Moon:** dry and close; the song carries the space
- **Home lawn:** soft, open, short

## Mix philosophy
- Dynamic range is preserved: the bus "glue" compression is removed, effects get light peak control only, and quiet stays quiet.
- Loudness: about -18 LUFS integrated with a -1 dBFS ceiling. That's slightly quieter overall than now, which is what gives the launch and the explosion their size. I can also deliver a louder laptop-friendly version.
- Sub-bass is reserved for the lights, ignition, liftoff, the relight and the explosion. Nowhere else.

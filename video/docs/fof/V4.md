# The Future of Freshness: opening film, V4 (1:27)

V3 was moody and looked generated. V4 is the opposite on purpose: fun, informative, and drawn by hand. There is no
generated footage in it. Every picture is built in the composition (`src/FutureOfFreshnessV4.tsx`) in the palette of
the site: Cloud Dancer, ink, teal, and the four platform colors. A bright, warm voice tells the story, and the words
on screen land on the words she says.

## Voice
Elizabeth Anne (ElevenLabs eleven_v4), one take, "bright, warm, smiling, conversational, brisk". Transcribed with
eleven_scribe_v1; `public/fof4/vo_words.json` holds the word timings and `public/fof4/lines.json` places each sentence
in the film. The composition and the mix read the same two files, so a change to a line's `at` moves picture and sound
together.

Script: "TheraBreath took away the burn. Now, let's give more people a reason to want it. Here's the math. Mouthwash is
in sixty-five of every hundred American homes. TheraBreath is in fourteen. That leaves eighty-six who haven't been
introduced yet. Flavor is how we say hello. In July, you came to Norco and watched us make flavor. Then Ross asked for
more: new flavor experiences that strengthen your appeal with loyal users, and attract new consumers to the franchise.
So: November ninth. Two hours. Four chapters. One Playbook. Chapter one, Signals. Seven of them. You vote for three.
Then Flavor School: how cooling works, and how flavor survives inside your formula. Chapter two, Territories. Four
platforms, and a blind tasting. Four cups, four letters, no names until you've tasted. Chapter three, Concepts. Two
teams build, tune on The Bench, pitch, and vote. Chapter four, Pipeline. Now, next, later. With owners and dates. And
by noon, a Flavor Playbook, with your names on the cover. The Future of Freshness. Let's build it together."

## Sound
Score: ElevenLabs Music v2.5, playful marimba and pizzicato at 118 BPM, sparse for the first eight bars, a lift at
bar 32 (under chapter four and the Playbook), a definite ending. Ducked under speech. Effects: four sounds made for
this cut (a soft pop, a glockenspiel ding, a page flip with a stamp, a marker swipe), pitched per cue, plus a few from
the house library. Mixed in `scripts/fof4_mix.py`; mastered to -16.5 LUFS, -2.5 dBTP.

```bash
npx remotion render src/index.ts FutureOfFreshnessV4 out/fof4/pic.mp4 --muted --public-dir=public_v2 --concurrency=4 --crf=16
python3 scripts/fof4_mix.py out/fof4/mix_raw.wav <ffmpeg>
ffmpeg -i out/fof4/mix_raw.wav -af "volume=3.0dB,alimiter=limit=0.72:attack=3:release=80:level=false,alimiter=limit=0.8:attack=1:release=50:level=false" out/fof4/mix_master.wav
```

# The Future of Freshness: opening film, V2

V1 was accurate but too pale, too static, and its type was too small. V2 keeps every fact and changes the film around them.

## Look: "Light through liquid, in the dark"
- Deep ink black (#04070B) instead of Cloud Dancer white. Crystal liquids lit from behind, so every drop carries a thin spectral edge.
- Macro, high-speed, slow motion. Things burst, crack, bloom and rise. Nothing just sits on a table.
- Color is earned: the first ten seconds are clear and cool; color floods in with "the want"; each platform gets its own saturated world; the finale resolves to clear light.
- Still no faces, bottles, packs or product claims. Platform worlds avoid any ingredient in the blind flight (no lemon, ginger, grapefruit, lavender, blossoms, berries or frozen pops).

## Type: big, kinetic, on the beat
- Newsreader display at 150 to 300 px, light weight, italic for the one word that matters. Geist Mono labels at 24 px or more.
- Moves: masked word rises with a blur that resolves; knockout words with footage inside the letters; numbers that count; a light sweep across titles; a short chromatic split on the hardest hits.
- Chapter cards: huge outlined numerals 01 to 04.

# The Future of Freshness: opening film, V3 ("The Drop")

V2 had the facts and the look. It did not have an idea that carried the whole film, and its thesis line went stale when
the workshop copy changed. V3 keeps every fact and every sourced line, changes the thesis, and builds the film around
one image.

## The idea
One drop of water lets go in the first three seconds and does not land until the title, 67 seconds later. Everything in
between happens inside it or on the way down: the burn, the want, the hundred homes, Norco, Ross's words, the seven
signals, four worlds refracted inside the same drop, the four cups, the bench, the Playbook. Twice the score cuts to
silence while the drop falls alone through the dark, then the hit lands on nothing: once for "November 9", once for the
landing. The crown splash that opened V2 is now the climax.

## What the research gave us
- Commit to one visual idea and one sonic drive (The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo: black ooze; Westworld: the machine
  making; True Detective: double exposure). V2 was a montage of twenty macro shots. V3 is one drop.
- Withhold. The best teasers decide what not to show (The Shining's elevator, Alien's glimpses). The drop never lands
  until the title; the samples stay blind; the voice never says OXYD-8.
- Shot length runs long to short; the sound arc is tension, build, barrage; a drop-out to near silence makes the next
  impact feel enormous, and the riser cuts to a hit on the downbeat.
- Type stays subordinate to the picture: a thin overline, one serif line at a time, left-aligned (Severance, Dragon
  Tattoo). The violence and beauty belong to the image, not the lettering.
- The cut should pass through the image, not across it: every chapter opens through a drop-shaped iris with a thin
  spectral rim, so the transition reads as light passing through the drop.

Sources: Art of the Title (Severance, The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo, True Detective, Westworld), trailer-editing and
trailer-sound guides (cold open, long-to-short pacing, riser into hit, the drop-out), Stash and Famous Campaigns brand
film roundups.

## Voice
V2 take for every unchanged line. New take (same voice, Elizabeth Anne, eleven_v4) for the opening pair:
"TheraBreath took away the burn." / "Now, let's give more people a reason to want it." The new line matches the Stage,
the site, the pre-brief and the Playbook.

## Sound
The V2 score (ElevenLabs Music v2.5, re-edited on its bar lines) moved with the picture, with two holes cut into it:
35.75 to 36.5 and 66.45 to 67.3. A single plink in each hole, then the impact. New landing: impact plus flood hit plus
shimmer. Mixed in `scripts/fof3_mix.py`, mastered to about -16 LUFS.

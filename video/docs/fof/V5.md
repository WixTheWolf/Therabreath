# The Future of Freshness: opening film, V5 (the pre-brief intro, 1:22)

V4 was a trailer for a morning that has since changed (The Bench and the pitches are gone, the names on the cover are
not promised anywhere) and it never asked anyone to do anything. V5 keeps V4's drawn world, palette, voice and footage
windows and rebuilds the story around what an attendee needs before November 9: why we're meeting, what the morning is,
what they'll do, what we'll leave with, and what to do now. Ross's goal is quoted whole, on screen and in the voice.

Two endings come from one take: the pre-brief cut (`FutureOfFreshnessV5Brief`, shipped as `opening.brief.*`) points at
the four questions on the same page, and the site cut (`FutureOfFreshnessV5Site`, `opening.*`) points at the pre-brief.

## Build
The footage (Higgsfield jobs in `jobs_v2.md` and `V3.md`; `crown_slow` and `ripples_slow` are those clips slowed 2x with
motion interpolation) and the audio (the ElevenLabs flow above) are not committed. `public_v5/` hardlinks just what V5
renders with.
```bash
python3 scripts/fof5_words.py <take> public/fof5/vo_words.json   # or ElevenLabs Scribe on the take
bash scripts/fof5_build.sh <take> public/fof5/vo_words.json --ship  # layout, both renders, mix, master, encode, copy
```
`scripts/fof5_layout.py` turns the take and its word times into `public/fof5/take_brief.json` and `take_site.json` (the
picture, the mix and the captions all read them) and `captions_*.vtt`, so a new take re-times everything. It cuts
between lines at the take's own pause (the longest silence near where the transcript puts the boundary): Scribe hands
the pause after a sentence to its last word and can run 0.1 to 0.2 s into the next one, so the transcript only says
where to look and the audio says where to cut.

## Reviewing the picture
`node scripts/fof5_stills.mjs <brief|site> <outdir> <t1> <t2> ...` writes stills at film times from one bundle (about 2 s each), for contact sheets.

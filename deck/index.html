Flavor culture is much bigger than mint.

Twelve places reshaping what fresh can mean. A vocabulary to borrow from, not a launch list.12 regions · 4 platforms · 1 room
Flavor culture is far bigger than traditional mint. These twelve places are where freshness cues are coming from, and every line runs back to this room. The point is not to launch twelve flavors. It is to borrow a vocabulary.
Borrowed vocabulary

What the world is teaching fresh.

A few lessons worth borrowing. In Kerala, cardamom after meals already means a fresh mouth. In Japan, yuzu and sencha show that fresh can be bright and quiet at once. Grasse shows how a rinse could be structured like a fragrance: top, heart and base. London’s zero-proof bars prove that sophisticated and alcohol-free go together, and TheraBreath already is both.

One rule to judge every idea today: keep one familiar freshness cue and change one thing people can notice. Arctic Yuzu keeps the cold, clean finish and changes the citrus. Green Tea Cucumber keeps gentle freshness and adds calm. Ginger Lime keeps the crisp cool and adds a spark of warmth.

Stretch one dimension at a time.

Here is the same idea as a map of the white space. Each spoke is a dimension, running from what oral care usually is at the center to where it could go at the edge. Classic mint sits near the center on all of them. Strong concepts pull hard on one or two spokes. When everything changes at once, there is nothing left to trust.
Four innovation platforms

Build worlds, not one-off flavors.

We think in platforms, not one-off flavors. Four territories TheraBreath could own across several launches: New Fresh, Sensory Proof, Ritual by Time and Elevated Escape. Every concept you will taste belongs to at least one. Later we will pick two.
PLATFORM 01

New Fresh

Expand what fresh tastes like, well beyond peppermint.

Cues
Bright fruit, citrus peel, tea, cool greens, soft herbs, botanicals.
Promise
The same clean TheraBreath finish, reached by a new route.
Concepts
Arctic Yuzu · Green Tea Cucumber · Grapefruit Rose Mint
New Fresh expands what fresh tastes like beyond peppermint: bright fruit, citrus peel, tea, cool greens and soft herbs, all orbiting a familiar mint anchor. Same clean TheraBreath finish, reached by a new route.
PLATFORM 02

Sensory Proof

Flavor and sensation that make freshness something you can feel happening.

Cues
Cooling bloom, bright top note, contrast, a flavor that evolves, a long clean finish.
Guardrail
Intensity without burn. Gentleness is the proof point, never the trade-off.
Concepts
Arctic Yuzu · Ginger Lime
Sensory Proof makes freshness perceptible: a bright opening, a cooling bloom, and a clean lingering finish. The guardrail is non-negotiable: intensity without burn. Gentleness is the proof point, never the trade-off.
PLATFORM 03

Ritual by Time

Different moments of the day call for different kinds of fresh.

24 hFive moments
Ritual by Time gives different flavors to different moments: a bright cold start in the morning, a calm reset midday, something to cut through coffee, a confident note before going out, and a soft wind-down at night.
PLATFORM 04

Elevated Escape

Premium flavor worlds borrowed from fragrance, spa, tea and zero-proof bars.

Cues
Fragrance structure, hospitality, culinary pairings, global botanicals.
Promise
A small daily luxury that is still clean and alcohol-free.
Concepts
Grapefruit Rose Mint · Pear Cardamom Mint
Elevated Escape borrows from fragrance, spa, tea and zero-proof bars. It uses fragrance logic, top, heart and base, but always finishes clean. A small daily luxury that stays alcohol-free.

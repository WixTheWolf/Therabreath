Getting ready to taste

A clean palate makes better calls.

Four small things that make the tasting more accurate for everyone in the room.

Skip coffee and mint for an hour

Coffee, strong food, gum and mouthwash linger and mask what you taste.

Go easy on fragrance

Perfume and cologne carry across the room and change how a rinse smells.

Tell us about sensitivities

If you have allergies or sensitivities, let us know before the day and we’ll plan around them.

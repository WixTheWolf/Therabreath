A note from The Flavor Factory

Dear TheraBreath team,

For decades, freshness in oral care has meant one thing: mint. Cold, clean, strong. It has served the category well. But the people who buy TheraBreath now live in a much bigger world of flavor. They order yuzu spritzes, drink matcha, choose skincare by the botanical, and wind down with chamomile.

On November 9 we'd like to show you what we see coming, let you taste six directions we've built for an oxygen-based rinse, and then hand the pen to you. By noon, we'll have written the TheraBreath Flavor Playbook together.

This pre-brief takes about five minutes. The only homework is at the end: tell us what your freshness feels like.

With thanks,
The Flavor Factory team

The idea in one line

Freshness is evolving.

Consumers now understand freshness through a far broader sensory vocabulary. The next era of oral care won't come from another mint. It will come from designing different kinds of freshness for different people, moments and needs.

Why we're meeting · the four questions you asked us
The two hours

Teach. Show. Taste. Discuss. Decide.

Nobody lectures for 45 minutes. We alternate: a little context, then the flavors, then your judgment.

    What you'll taste

    Six territories. Six sealed flavors.

    Each territory is a direction freshness could take, with a hero concept built for TheraBreath's rinse base. The names stay sealed until you taste them. Tap a card for a hint.

    Your five minutes of homework

    What does your freshness feel like?

    Halfway through the session we'll stop presenting and ask the room to vote on these same five lines. Set your own answer now, save it, and bring your instinct into the room.

    Your answers stay on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    About the tasting

    The palate is the presentation.

    • Six flavor conceptsEach is prepared in a rinse base and served in small cups. These are flavor explorations, not finished products.
    • Rinse, then expectorateSwish for a few seconds and spit. Water and plain crackers will be on the table between samples.
    • Come with a clean palateIf you can, skip coffee and mint gum in the hour before we start.
    • Tell us about sensitivitiesIf you have allergies or would rather not taste, let us know ahead of time. There's plenty to do without tasting.

Objective 01 · Trends

Trends shaping the future of oral care.

Flavor, sensory and consumer trends we see across the categories we make flavors for, from beverages to nutraceuticals. They are shaping what people expect a rinse to feel like.

Consumer trend
Beauty

Personal-care rituals

Oral care is joining the skincare shelf as one more step in a self-care routine.

Where we see itLayered skincare routines, bathroom “shelfies”, oral care sold beside serums.
Flavor trend
Flavor

A broader vocabulary

People now taste yuzu, shiso and cardamom in drinks, snacks and wellness shots every week.

Where we see itZero-proof bars, functional beverages, global menus, fine fragrance.
Sensory trend
Sensation

Sensory proof

People want an effect they can feel and describe. That is how they know it works.

Where we see itCooling skincare, tingling lip products, “you can feel it working” claims.
Consumer trend
Wellness

Everyday wellness

Products earn a place in the day: morning energy, midday reset, evening calm.

Where we see itSleep and hydration rituals, adaptogen drinks, “night” versions of everything.

How this experience works

What this is

The in-room companion to the deck, and the leave-behind afterwards. It holds the tools we use during the session: the blind scorecard and rinse timer, the Flavor Lab (our Create tool), the decision board, the pipeline and the draft playbook.

The session runs 10:00 AM to noon in Darwin, then lunch. The order follows the four objectives in Ross Conroy’s invite: trends, territories, concepts, pipeline. Within that, we taste blind first, then reveal what was behind each code, then create and decide. Don’t open the reveal section before the tasting.

Type

Archivo ExtraBold, expanded
Headlines. Heavy, wide and confident, and readable from the back of the room.

Figtree
Body copy at 18–20 px, medium weight, high contrast.

Colour

TheraBreath blue and navy carry the brand. The orange of the cap is the one accent: it marks what is active, chosen or next. Each flavor and platform adds its own world colour behind the content. Coral means overreach and appears only there.

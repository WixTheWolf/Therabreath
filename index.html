The world around TheraBreath

What people call fresh is changing.

Four signals from outside oral care are reshaping what people expect a rinse to feel like.

Beauty

Personal-care rituals

Oral care is joining the skincare shelf as one more step in a self-care routine.

Seen inLayered skincare routines, bathroom “shelfies”, oral care sold beside serums.
Flavor

A broader vocabulary

People now taste yuzu, shiso and cardamom in drinks, snacks and wellness shots every week.

Seen inZero-proof bars, functional beverages, global menus, fine fragrance.

Notes for the presenting team

How this experience works

Idea

One bottle, many worlds. The TheraBreath bottle, orange cap and all, anchors the story. The colour field behind the page eases from one world to the next as each scene reaches the middle of the screen. In the concept section the bottle stays pinned while its liquid, label and world change for each flavor.

Each screen answers one question: a big idea, one supporting line, one piece of evidence. The presenter talks and the screen shows.

Type

Archivo ExtraBold, expanded
Headlines. Heavy, wide and confident, and readable from the back of the room.

Figtree
Body copy at 18–20 px, medium weight, high contrast.

Colour

TheraBreath blue and navy carry the brand. The orange of the cap is the one accent: it marks what is active, chosen or next. Each flavor and platform adds its own world colour behind the content. Coral means overreach and appears only there.

Presenting

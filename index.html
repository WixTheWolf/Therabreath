01 / Our partnership

A strong partnership, ready for scale.

Therabreath asked for a capabilities review because the brand is growing. This visit is our chance to show the full system behind the work: the room, the people, the planning, the quality discipline, and the flavor thinking that keep the business moving.

The goal is simple: make it obvious that The Flavor Factory is prepared for the next stage of Therabreath growth.

99%+

Reliable supply and consistent execution — the stuff that only gets noticed when it is missing.

Dedicated

A dedicated Therabreath production space so the important things stay controlled and repeatable.

Growth

A multi-year relationship with momentum, trust, and more room to build.

02 / Visit agenda

Show the system, not just the samples.

Their note was clear: they value the partnership, and they want to make sure both teams are positioned for success as Therabreath continues to scale.

The visit is a capabilities review.

R&D and Procurement will be in the room. They want an open, workshop-style discussion around resiliency, innovation, operations, and how TFF sees itself supporting Therabreath over the next 3-5 years.

This is not just a tasting. The tasting matters, but the bigger job is to build confidence.

What we need to make obvious.

  • ScaleCapacity planning, redundancy, and room to grow with the brand.
  • ControlQuality systems, compliance, sourcing, and business continuity.
  • IdeasFlavor creation, sensory science, trends, and co-creation capacity.
  • TrustClear communication, transparency, and long-term investment.

04 / Flavor trends

Fresh can stretch further.

Mint is still the anchor. The opportunity is to bring in more reasons to use the product: comfort, brightness, hydration, a little fun, and the occasional seasonal idea.

01

Functional + sensory pleasure

Fresh works harder when people enjoy using the product.

02

Beyond mint

Dessert, tropical, and calming profiles can open new doors without walking away from fresh.

03

Natural-forward profiles

Cleaner cues need flavors that feel clear, bright, and intentional.

04

Kids and adult indulgence

There is room for both family-friendly and more adult routines.

05

Limited-edition energy

Limited runs can create a reason to try now.
